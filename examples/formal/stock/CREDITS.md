# Example footage

`clouds.mp4` is a 14-second excerpt of **Clouds (time lapse)** by [Editor](https://www.youtube.com/user/Editor), originally published on 28 May 2011.

- [Source and license record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Clouds_(time_lapse).webm)
- [Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AI3n41WXdK0)
- [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)

Changes: excerpted, resized to 1280 × 720, audio removed, re-encoded as H.264. The sample documentary further crops this illustrative footage into its teaching panel and adds narration, captions and teaching graphics. No endorsement by the creator is implied.

This small library is for the included cloud/water-cycle examples. Configure your own licensed library or Pexels for other subjects. The application does not substitute unrelated footage when a search misses.
